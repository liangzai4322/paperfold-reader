Paperfold Book is a static Latin subset of Source Serif 4 from Adobe.
https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif

Paperfold Song is a static subset of Noto Serif SC from Google.
https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk

Paperfold Kai is a subset of LXGW WenKai v1.530.
https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai/releases/tag/v1.530

Each font is renamed and converted to WOFF2. The Chinese subsets contain
GB2312 and common Traditional Chinese characters. The device supplies fallback
glyphs for other characters. Original SIL Open Font License notices are included
in the corresponding OFL files.
